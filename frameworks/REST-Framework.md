# The REST Framework™

A physician-designed leadership sustainability infrastructure. The foundation of everything Dr. Renee builds, sells, speaks, and writes.

## What It Is

A structured, measurable, physician-led system that builds the capacity of an organization's most experienced, most trusted, most irreplaceable leaders to perform at the highest level without depleting the people doing it. Not a wellness program. A performance infrastructure.

## The Four Dimensions (Ecosystem Version)

| Dimension | Focus |
|-----------|-------|
| RECOGNIZE | Identify early warning signs of depletion in self, team, and organization. |
| STRENGTHEN | Build and protect leadership performance capacity. |
| SUSTAIN | Create organizational and personal sustainability infrastructure. |
| TRANSFORM | Shift leadership identity toward genuine sustainability. |

## The Four Steps (Content Version)

- **R — Recognize** early signs of workforce fatigue and burnout exposure.
- **E — Explore** leadership drivers and root causes.
- **S — Support** systems, culture, and mental health infrastructure.
- **T — Take Action** with practical, measurable steps.

## Why It Is Different

Built from 26 years inside operating rooms, the highest-stakes performance environment that exists. It combines medical expertise, workforce sustainability, leadership psychology, wellness science, executive strategy, and human-centered performance systems into one practical framework for high-pressure environments. The obstacles most engagements hit (resistance to change, competing priorities, budget-only conversations, cultural inertia that mistakes depletion for dedication) were built into the design from the start.

## The Assessment Layer

REST Leadership Sustainability Assessment™ — 25 physician-designed questions across all 4 dimensions. Produces a Leadership Sustainability Score™ on a 125-point scale across 5 clinical ranges: Sustaining (100 to 125), Stabilizing (75 to 99), Depleting (50 to 74), Depleted (25 to 49), Critical (under 25).

## How It Shows Up in Engagements

Diagnostic (Assess + Strategize), Implementation (adds facilitated workshops and leader coaching), and Transformation (adds a 12-month physician-embedded partnership with monitoring, certification, and a custom REST Leadership Playbook). See [Programs and Offers](../wiki/04-programs-and-offers.md).
