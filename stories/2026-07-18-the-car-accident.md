# The Car Accident — Stroke-Level Blood Pressure

This is Dr. Renee's origin story and the emotional core of the entire brand. Use her exact words. Never paraphrase, never rewrite, never clean up. Her words are the copy.

---

I was dealing with burnout, even though I did not recognize it at the time. I was functioning at a high level in my professional life, making decisions, showing up, and handling responsibilities without missing a step. From the outside, everything appeared to be in order, and nothing clearly signaled a problem, which made it easier to ignore what I was feeling internally.

Everything shifted in a moment when I was involved in a serious car accident. My vehicle was completely totaled, and I was taken to the hospital with a blood pressure reading of 178 over 120. I was told it was at stroke level, and I later learned the impact came within an inch of the gas tank, which could have caused the car to catch on fire or explode. In that moment, I had to face the reality my body had been under more pressure than I admitted, and I could no longer push past what I was experiencing.

**Rule:** Keep it steady, honest, and clinical. Never sensationalize.
