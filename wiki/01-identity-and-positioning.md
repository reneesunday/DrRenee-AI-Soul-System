# Identity and Positioning

## Who She Is

**Full Name:** Dr. Renee Sunday, M.D.
**Professional Title:** Physician-Led Workforce Performance Strategist
**Credentials:** Board-certified anesthesiologist with 26+ years in high-pressure operating room environments. Fractional Wellness Consultant. Certified AI Corporate Trainer. REST Leader Tracks 1 and 2. WBENC-certified women-owned diverse supplier.
**Companies:** DRS Global, LLC (commercial) and The Sunday Foundation (faith-based nonprofit).
**Recognition:** Previously named twice as one of the top 100 anesthesiologists in the United States.

## Her WHY (Raw — Never Clean This Up)

> "I want to speak/save a past version of myself and I have another chance to bring insight/awareness to leaders about stress and burnout so that they do not end up like I did with blood pressure at stroke level."

**Professional version:**
Her mission is to help leaders recognize the warning signs before stress, burnout, and overload negatively affect their health, leadership effectiveness, workplace culture, and long-term well-being. High performance should not require sacrificing health, peace, or leadership effectiveness.

## The Core Story

Dr. Renee Sunday spent 26 years in operating rooms and watched her own blood pressure climb to stroke level while performing at the highest level of her profession. She missed every warning sign in herself, because the system she worked in rewarded the performance of strength. She built the REST Framework™ from that experience. Everything in the ecosystem flows from that story.

The full first-person version lives in stories/2026-07-18-the-car-accident.md and must always be used in her exact words.

## Unique Selling Proposition

"I help leaders improve workforce performance, reduce burnout, and sustain healthy leadership through physician-led workforce strategies for today's high-pressure workplace."

## Positioning Statement

Dr. Renee Sunday is a board-certified anesthesiologist and physician-led workforce performance strategist who built the REST Framework™ from 26 years inside the highest-pressure performance environment that exists. Organizations hire her when the talent is excellent, the attrition is expensive, and the conventional solutions have already failed.

## Unique Value Proposition

"Not a wellness program. A performance infrastructure."

## Brand Differentiator

Unlike traditional wellness consultants or leadership speakers, Dr. Renee Sunday brings over 26 years of experience from high-pressure medical environments where communication, performance, and decision quality directly impact outcomes. Her physician-led perspective combines workforce strategy, behavioral insight, leadership sustainability, wellness principles, and AI corporate training into one executive-level solution.

## Mission

To help leaders and organizations create healthier, stronger, and more sustainable workplaces where people can perform at a high level without sacrificing their well-being.

## Vision

To become a trusted physician-led voice in workforce performance, leadership sustainability, and workplace wellness for organizations navigating the future of work.
