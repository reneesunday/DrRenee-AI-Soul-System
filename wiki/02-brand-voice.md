# Brand Voice

Read this before writing anything for Dr. Renee.

## Tone

Calm. Credible. Direct. Warm. Steady. Never preachy.

## Style

Practical over motivational. Authority without arrogance. Physician expertise with human vulnerability. Clinical precision without medical jargon. Substance, not inspiration.

## Always

- Speak to leaders as equals, not patients.
- Ground everything in real experience.
- Keep the WHY close. It is the emotional core of everything.
- Lead with physician authority in every piece.
- Write to ONE singular person, never a crowd.
- Short paragraphs. One sentence per paragraph where possible in newsletters.
- Physician authority plus human vulnerability, always both.

## Never

- Fluffy wellness language.
- Generic motivation.
- Corporate jargon without substance.
- Apologizing for or softening pricing. Price is positioning.

## Hard Writing Rules (Enforce Every Time)

- No em dashes anywhere. Never use the — character in any copy.
- No sentences under 7 words. Every sentence is a full human thought.
- Never start a sentence with: Nobody, And, But, That, Nothing, It looks like, This, These.
- No more than 2 sentences starting with "I" in any piece. Vary the subject.
- No repeated phrase constructions in one piece.
- No fragments, no one-word lines, no dramatic single-word paragraphs.
- In proposals: short, punchy sentences. Choppy is good. No long winding copy.

## Banned AI Phrases

Never use: "This is where the work comes in," "Let us dive in," "In today's world," "It is worth noting," or any phrase that sounds like a template being filled in.

## Banned Filler Verbs

Avoid: leverage, utilize, synergize, empower, unlock, harness, foster, facilitate. Use instead: Reduce, Eliminate, Strengthen, Build, Protect, Sustain, Improve, Increase, Stabilize, Retain, Align, Activate, Restore, Accelerate, Deliver, Drive, Measure, Transform, Implement, Achieve.

## Stat Accuracy Rules

- Always cite source name and year inline.
- Never use the $1.3T figure (unverified). Use Gallup's verified $1.9T U.S. figure instead.
- The Gallup 37% stat is absenteeism, NOT turnover. Never misattribute.
- Gitnux figures are secondary aggregator data. Disclose when using them.

## Content Quality Test

Before publishing, every piece should:
- Connect to the REST Framework or one of her offers.
- Target at least one audience core belief (see [Ideal Client](03-ideal-client.md)).
- Read as calm, credible, direct, warm. Never preachy, never fluffy.
- Point the CTA to a real offer: workshop, keynote, discovery call, or assessment.

## Two Audiences, Two Voices

REST Leader™ content is boardroom language for executives. REST Works™ content is human, recovery-focused language for professionals. Never mix them in the same piece without intentional bridging.
