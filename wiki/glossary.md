# Glossary

Dr. Renee's own language. Terms she has coined and words that mean something specific in her world.

## The REST Framework™

Her signature physician-led framework. Two versions of the acronym appear in her materials and both are hers to use depending on context:

**Ecosystem / four-dimension version:**
- **RECOGNIZE** — Identify early warning signs of depletion in self, team, and organization.
- **STRENGTHEN** — Build and protect leadership performance capacity.
- **SUSTAIN** — Create organizational and personal sustainability infrastructure.
- **TRANSFORM** — Shift leadership identity toward genuine sustainability.

**Content-creator version:**
- **R — Recognize** early signs of workforce fatigue and burnout exposure.
- **E — Explore** leadership drivers and root causes.
- **S — Support** systems, culture, and mental health infrastructure.
- **T — Take Action** with practical, measurable steps.
