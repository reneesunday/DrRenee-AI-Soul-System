# Dreams

Her big vision lives here. Not the next 90-day goal. The real thing.

## The Vision

To become a trusted physician-led voice in workforce performance, leadership sustainability, and workplace wellness for organizations navigating the future of work. To help leaders and organizations build healthier, stronger, more sustainable workplaces where people perform at a high level without sacrificing their well-being.

## The Deeper WHY

She wants to speak to and save a past version of herself. She had blood pressure at stroke level and missed every warning sign. This is a second chance to bring insight and awareness to leaders before stress and burnout cost them their health. High performance should never require sacrificing health, peace, or leadership effectiveness.

## Revenue Goal

$500,000+ annually.

Path options: one enterprise license at $500,000/year, or two 12-month fractional partnerships, or five on-site full-day workshops, or one fractional plus two full-day workshops, or five mid-size organizational licenses.

## The Movement Layer

REST For All™, powered by The Sunday Foundation, is the legacy. Every product that serves a boardroom funds physician-prescribed wellness reaching a community that could never afford it. This throughline makes the ecosystem a movement, not just a business.

## Words of the Year

Alignment (personal). ACTIVATED (business stage).

## What Life Looks Like When It All Works

A stable, physician-led company with a team running the systems she built. Leaders and healthcare organizations across the country carrying the REST Framework™ inside their walls. Her story preventing in others what it nearly cost her. Faith, family, speaking stages, and a foundation that reaches people who could never afford the boardroom price.
