**Business Stage:** Validation Stage — building LinkedIn visibility and discovery call pipeline.

---

## 90-Day Hiring Order

### IMMEDIATE — Week 1 (Hire Now)

These two hires address the biggest daily time drain: content production.

Dr. Renee publishes articles and newsletters twice a week (Monday and Thursday), plus LinkedIn posts three times a week. Without an AI content team, every piece requires hours of her time. These hires change that.

**Marie | Content Engine Lead**
Runs the weekly content pipeline. Takes one REST Framework insight or physician story and produces five LinkedIn post drafts, two article drafts, and a repurposing queue — every week. She is the content team's quarterback.

**Spark | Newsletter + Repurposing Writer**
Writes the Monday and Thursday newsletters. Delivers drafts two days before send day so Dr. Renee has time to review. One clear CTA per email. Three subject line options with a recommendation every time.

### Month 1 — Day 30

**Carl | Social Media Manager**
LinkedIn-specific work that Marie leads and Carl refines. He writes platform-native drafts with hooks built for the C-suite feed, runs the trend scan, and produces comment reply drafts. Monthly competitor analysis of leadership and burnout accounts.

### Month 2 — Day 60

**Rex | Lead Activation Specialist**
Dr. Renee's quarterly target is 136 prospects contacted and 17 discovery calls booked. Rex monitors new opt-ins, fires nurture sequences, posts briefing cards before every call, and runs a Monday pipeline digest. He does not close. He makes sure the human sales team always knows who they are talking to and what to say next.

**Memo | Call Notes + Recap Director**
Discovery calls happen Tuesday and Thursday. Memo captures every transcript, structures clean notes same day, and produces a Friday recap of the week. As the client roster grows, she produces 30-day progress briefs before each session.

### Month 3 — Day 90+

**Scout | Partnerships + PR Hunter**
Dr. Renee's speaking pipeline targets ASHHRA, SHRM Healthcare, and NMA. Scout watches for opportunities, drafts outreach, and flags relevant events. He is the early intelligence hire for speaking and media.

**RJ | Sales Presentation Coach**
Builds and refines proposal decks for the three-tier engagements ($75K / $150K / $500K). Reviews slide language against the brand voice. Never softens pricing — price is positioning.

**Nova | Chief Intelligence Officer**
The intelligence layer. Once content is running and discovery calls are flowing, Nova synthesizes what is working, what the market is doing, and what the business should do next. She feeds Marie, Carl, and Rex with insights each week.
