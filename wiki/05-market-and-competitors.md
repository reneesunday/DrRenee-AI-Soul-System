# Market and Competitors

## The Problem She Solves

Burnout-driven voluntary attrition rooted in extraction-based performance culture. Organizations reward the performance of strength until their most experienced leaders depart, disengage, or break down.

## The Unique Angle

The only physician-led workforce performance framework built from 26 years inside the highest-stakes clinical environments that exist. This is the moat. Dr. Renee is not a coach or a consultant who studied burnout. She is a board-certified physician who lived it at stroke level.

## How She Is Different

Traditional wellness vendors offer programs. Dr. Renee offers performance infrastructure. "Not a wellness program. A performance infrastructure." Leadership speakers offer motivation. Dr. Renee offers physician-designed, measurable, evidence-informed systems. Generic corporate wellness does not understand healthcare culture. Dr. Renee speaks the language of the operating room and the boardroom.

## What Clients Are Told by Others in the Space

Wellness vendors hand out a candle and a breathing exercise. Leadership development programs focus on productivity or motivation without addressing sustainability at the structural level. Most solutions treat burnout as a personal weakness instead of an organizational warning sign. This is the exact gap Dr. Renee fills.

## Status Quo Cost (The ROI Story)

A 1,000-person org at 20% attrition = $15M to $30M/year in replacement costs. Replacing one employee = 50% to 200% of annual salary (SHRM). A Chief of Medicine earning $500,000 who burns out or departs costs $500,000 to $1,000,000 in replacement costs. A Director of Nursing earning $180,000 carries a replacement cost up to $360,000.

Healthcare-specific: RN turnover costs hospitals $3.9M to $5.8M annually. One 1% increase in RN turnover costs approximately $289,000 to $295,000 annually. Physician burnout costs the U.S. $4.6 billion annually. Nearly 40% of nurses intend to leave by 2029.

The math is the pitch: the status quo is the most expensive option on the table.

## Key Market Statistics (Verified)

- 82% of senior leaders experienced exhaustion indicative of burnout (Deloitte Canada and LifeWorks, 2022).
- 51% of senior leaders have contemplated leaving their roles (Deloitte Canada and LifeWorks, 2022).
- Burned-out leaders are 3.5 times more likely to leave to improve well-being (DDI, 2025).
- Managers are 36% more likely than non-managers to report burnout (meQuilibrium, 2024).
- Not-engaged and actively disengaged U.S. employees account for approximately $1.9 trillion in lost productivity (Gallup, 2024). Never use the unverified $1.3T figure.
- Employee disengagement and attrition could cost a median-size S&P 500 company $228M to $355M per year (McKinsey, 2023).
- Burnout prevalence is 58% lower among employees with strong manager support (meQuilibrium, 2024).
