# Ideal Client

## Ideal Client Profile (Primary)

**The Overloaded Executive.** Age 38 to 58, earning $150K to $500K+, responsible for teams and culture, performing externally while quietly exhausted internally. Has tried motivation and discipline. Needs practical physician-led systems.

## ICP for Corporate Sales

CHROs, CMOs, VP Workforce Development, and Director Employee Experience at healthcare systems (500+ employees) and corporations with diverse supplier mandates.

## Primary Audiences

- Corporate Leaders and C-Suite Executives
- HR Directors and Chief People Officers
- Healthcare Organizations (hospitals, medical groups, healthcare systems)
- Government and Public Sector Leaders
- Workforce Development Leaders
- High-performing professionals operating in high-pressure environments

## Who "Leaders" Means in Proposals

C-suite executives, VP-level leaders, Department Directors, Physician Leaders (Chief of Medicine, Medical Directors), and Nurse Leaders (CNO, Directors of Nursing). These are NOT frontline physicians and nurses. These are the people leading them. Every departure begins with a leader who stopped being sustained.

## Buyer Personas (Healthcare)

### Persona 1 — The Mission-Driven Hospital Executive
Chief Medical Officer, age 52, regional or large hospital system. Pain: physician turnover costing $500K to $1.3M per departure, nurse leader fatigue, personally running on empty but cannot admit it. Buys because she speaks his language and REST is built for high-pressure healthcare, not generic corporate settings. Triggering event: a key physician leader announced a departure, or a personal health scare he is quietly ignoring.

### Persona 2 — The Burned-Out Nurse Executive
Chief Nursing Officer / VP Patient Care Services, age 47, Southeast or Midwest hospital system. Pain: managing hundreds of nurses, absorbing the weight of staffing shortages, tired of wellness workshops that hand out a candle and a breathing exercise. Buys because Dr. Renee is a physician who understands the environment from the inside. Triggering event: a nurse leader resigned, or an alarming staff satisfaction survey.

### Persona 3 — The HR / Workforce Development Director
Director of Workforce Development and Employee Experience, age 41, large hospital system or academic medical center. Pain: has the data but cannot get leadership buy-in, needs a physician-led voice for boardroom credibility. Buys because physician credibility gives her program instant authority and REST is evidence-informed. Triggering event: board flagged workforce stability as a top-3 strategic risk, or CFO asked for a retention ROI report.

## Decision-Maker Job Titles

CMO (clinical authority + budget), CNO (operational authority), CEO (strategic + budget approval), CHRO (program authority), CPO (employee experience authority), VP/Director Workforce Development (procurement), VP/Director Employee Experience (procurement), Director Organizational Development (procurement), Hospital Administrator (operational), Department Medical Director (internal champion).

## Target Company Profiles

| Type | Size | Primary Need |
|------|------|-------------|
| Regional Hospital Systems | 500 to 5,000 employees | Nurse turnover, physician fatigue, leadership instability |
| Academic Medical Centers | 2,000 to 20,000 employees | Physician burnout, administrative overload, leadership succession |
| Multi-Hospital Health Systems | 5,000 to 50,000 employees | Enterprise workforce crisis, retention costs in the millions |
| Large Medical Groups | 100 to 500 physicians | Physician burnout, decision fatigue, revenue impact |
| Government / VA Healthcare | Any size | Chronic workforce stress, compliance-driven wellness |

## Triggering Events

New CHRO hired, failed engagement survey, mass attrition event, Q4 budget cycle, Joint Commission citation, high-profile departure, sentinel/medical error where leadership fatigue is a factor, peer system launching a wellness initiative, seeing Dr. Renee speak or reading her LinkedIn content, new fiscal year budget planning, CFO requesting a retention ROI analysis.
