## Reference — Stat-Led Newsletter Pattern (82% Burnout)

**Platform:** Newsletter / Email
**Description:** The proven 18-step stat-led structure, anchored on "82% of Senior Leaders Are Showing Signs of Burnout" (Deloitte Canada and LifeWorks, 2022).

The pattern: bold stat headline, source sentence, who should care, humanize the stat, outside-vs-inside contrast, the organizational mistake, why surface fixes fail, the shift required, the bridge to her help, what she offers, what the solutions do, the reframe, cost of inaction, the invitation, the close, the CTA, her three-title sign-off, and the source line.

**Why it worked:** It leads with credible physician-relevant data, names the reader's exact experience, reframes burnout as an organizational warning sign instead of personal weakness, and quantifies the cost of inaction before making the ask. No bullet points. All flowing prose. Calm, credible, direct.

## Reference — Monday Barnum Statement Opener

**Platform:** LinkedIn / Social
**Description:** "You can be performing at a high level and still be burning out. The body keeps score even when the calendar does not show it."

**Why it worked:** It is a truth leaders feel but have not said out loud. It mirrors her own story without naming it, and it invites a validation CTA like "Type REST if this resonates."
